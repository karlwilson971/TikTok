### 3-Tier Commission Structure
1. **Tier 1: Open Collaboration (15% Commission)**
   - Open to all qualified TikTok Shop creators with >1,000 followers.
   - Self-serve sample requests (requires 5 relevant posts in last 30 days).
2. **Tier 2: Targeted Performance Creators (20% Commission + Free Product)**
   - Actively recruited creators with 10k - 250k followers and consistent >5k view averages.
   - Customized script briefs provided by HookMaster.
3. **Tier 3: Elite Brand Ambassadors (25% Commission + Cash Bonuses + Spark Code Access)**
   - Creators who generate >$2,500 GMV/month.
   - Dedicated monthly sample drops, personalized discount codes, and Spark Ad revenue share.

## 4. Creator Brief & Content Guardrails

To protect brand reputation and maximize conversion, every seeded creator receives a 1-page digital brief:

### The "Do"s:
- **Show the product in action** in the first 2 seconds (unboxing, clicking, testing).
- **Use natural lighting and clear audio**.
- **Anchor the TikTok Shop yellow basket** directly to the specific product SKU.
- **Pin a top comment** answering a common question or prompting viewer discussion.

### The "Don't"s:
- **Do not read a dry feature list** like an infomercial.
- **Do not make medical, unverified, or permanent claims** that violate TikTok Shop terms.
- **Do not cover the yellow shopping cart icon** with on-screen text or stickers.
