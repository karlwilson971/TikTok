## 1. The 7-Stage Commercial Funnel Architecture

Every viewer journeys through a defined 7-stage commercial conversion sequence. `ShopGrowth` monitors each micro-conversion velocity and drop-off rate:

$$\text{Stage 1: Views} \longrightarrow \text{Stage 2: Profile Visits} \longrightarrow \text{Stage 3: Product Clicks} \longrightarrow \text{Stage 4: Add to Cart} \longrightarrow \text{Stage 5: Checkout} \longrightarrow \text{Stage 6: Purchase} \longrightarrow \text{Stage 7: Repeat Purchase}$$

```
┌───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┐
│ FUNNEL STAGE              │ WHAT OCCURS                                │ PRIMARY BENCHMARK          │
├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Video Views            │ Viewer stops scrolling on 3s hook          │ > 65% 2s Retention         │
│ 2. Profile Visits         │ Viewer explores brand account / pinned UGC │ 1.5% – 3.0% of Views       │
│ 3. Product Clicks (CTR)   │ Viewer taps in-video yellow cart / showcase│ 2.0% – 4.5% of Views       │
│ 4. Add to Cart (ATC)      │ Shopper selects SKU variant or bundle      │ 18% – 28% of Product Clicks│
│ 5. Initiated Checkout     │ Shopper enters TikTok Shop checkout flow   │ 65% – 80% of ATCs          │
│ 6. Completed Purchase     │ Payment confirmed (1-Click in-app checkout)│ 3.0% – 5.5% Product CVR    │
│ 7. Repeat Purchase (60d)  │ Customer repurchases consumable or bundle  │ 20% – 35% of Customers     │
└───────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. 18-Point Core Metric Taxonomy & Telemetry

`ShopGrowth` tracks 18 critical performance metrics to evaluate content and creator ROI:

### Top-of-Funnel Attention Metrics
1. **Video Views:** Total impression volume across organic and creator posts.
2. **3-Second Retention Rate:** Percentage of viewers watching past second 3.0 (Target: $\ge 65\%$).
3. **Average Watch Time (AWT):** Average seconds watched relative to total duration (Target: $\ge 50\%$).
4. **Completion Rate:** Percentage of viewers watching 100% of video (Target: $\ge 18\%$).

### Mid-Funnel Resonance & Social Proof Metrics
5. **Shares:** High-intent viral signal to algorithm (Target: $\ge 1.0\%$ of views).
6. **Saves:** High-intent buying bookmark signal (Target: $\ge 2.5\%$ of views).
7. **Comments:** Community feedback and objection pool (Target: $\ge 0.8\%$ of views).
8. **Follower Conversion Rate:** Viewers converting to profile followers (Target: $\ge 1.2\%$).

### Commercial Conversion Metrics
9. **Product Clicks (Yellow Cart Clicks):** Absolute clicks into the TikTok Shop product page.
10. **In-Video Product CTR:** Product Clicks / Total Views (Target: $2.0\% - 4.5\%$).
11. **Add-to-Cart Rate (ATC %):** Add to Cart events / Product Page Views (Target: $\ge 22\%$).
12. **Purchase Conversion Rate (CVR %):** Completed Orders / Product Page Clicks (Target: $\ge 3.4\%$).

### Financial & Lifetime Value (LTV) Metrics
13. **Total Revenue (GMV):** Gross merchandise value generated.
14. **Gross Profit & Contribution Margin:** Revenue minus COGS, shipping, TikTok fees (6%), and creator commissions (Target: $\ge 28\%$ Net Margin).
15. **Average Order Value (AOV):** Revenue / Total Orders (Target: $\ge \$42.00$ via bundle incentives).
16. **Refund / Return Rate:** Cancelled or returned orders (Target: $< 1.5\%$).
17. **Creator & Affiliate Revenue:** Proportion of GMV driven by external creators (Target: $40\% - 60\%$).
18. **Revenue per 1,000 Views (RPM / RPMV):** $\frac{\text{Total Revenue}}{\text{Total Views}} \times 1,000$ (Target: $\ge \$25.00 - \$45.00$).

## 4. The Affiliate & Creator Acquisition Engine

`ShopGrowth` builds and manages an army of creators who post authentic daily videos linked to the TikTok Shop showcase.

### Creator Segmentation & Selection (Fit Over Followers)
1. **Micro-Creators (10K–100K Followers) [Primary Scale Engine]:** High comment engagement, authentic niche authority, fast sample turnaround, responsive DMs.
2. **TikTok Shop Affiliates (Active Shop Posters):** Experienced with yellow cart tagging, familiar with TikTok affiliate commission payouts.
3. **UGC Creators (User Generated Content Pros):** High-production video quality, crisp audio foley, skilled at delivering Hook A/B/C variations.
4. **Niche Authorities (Tech/EDC/Organization):** Deep credibility; their endorsement overcomes purchase skepticism instantly.
5. **Lifestyle Vloggers (Morning Routine / CleanTok):** Seamlessly embed the product into organic aesthetic daily routines.

### Multi-Tier Commission Matrix
```
┌───────────────┬───────────────────────────────┬─────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TIER          │ CREATOR PROFILE               │ COMMISSION RATE │ INCENTIVES & PERKS                       │
├───────────────┼───────────────────────────────┼─────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 1 (Open) │ Open Affiliate Network (1K+)  │ 15% Base        │ Standard affiliate dashboard access      │
│ Tier 2 (Core) │ Vetted Micro-Creators (10K+)  │ 20% Standard    │ Free sample kit + 1-page hook brief      │
│ Tier 3 (VIP)  │ Top GMV Drivers & Authorities │ 25% VIP Bounty  │ Custom discount code + Spark Ad bonus    │
└───────────────┴───────────────────────────────┴─────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Creator Performance Ledger & Resource Allocation Rules
- **Scale Investment:** Creators generating $> \$1,000$ GMV/month with CVR $> 3.0\%$ $\rightarrow$ Upgrade to Tier 3 VIP (25%), send new product prototypes, whitelist for Spark Ads.
- **Maintain & Coach:** Creators with high views ($> 20\text{K}$) but low clicks $\rightarrow$ Provide high-converting Hook A/B/C briefs and sample unboxing scripts.
- **Trim & Discontinue:** Creators with vanity views, zero sales, or non-posting sample recipients $\rightarrow$ Cut sample allocations and reallocate budget to top performers.
