## 2. The 3-Tier Evidence Classification Matrix

Every marketing phrase and product benefit must be categorized into one of three strict tiers:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ EVIDENCE TIER                        │ DEFINITION & REGULATORY STANDARD                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. SUPPORTED CLAIM (GREEN)           │ Direct replication of peer-reviewed human clinical trial  │
│                                      │ findings using identical active dosages and forms.        │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. MARKETING INTERPRETATION (YELLOW) │ Logical contextualization of a physiological mechanism.   │
│                                      │ Requires explicit qualifier and clear context.            │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. UNSUPPORTED CLAIM (RED - VETO)    │ Exaggerated cure/treatment claims, unrealistic timelines, │
│                                      │ animal-only extrapolated claims, or deceptive promises.   │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 🚫 Non-Negotiable Prohibitions
- Strictly **ZERO** claims of diagnosing, treating, curing, or preventing any medical disease.
- Strictly **ZERO** fabricated doctor endorsements, fake before/after photos, or staged customer reviews.
- Strictly **ZERO** proprietary blends masking under-dosed active ingredients.

## 3. Public Batch Verification & CoA Transparency

Every production batch of Performance Lab products is tested by ISO-17025 accredited third-party laboratories for:
1. **Purity & Assay:** Verifies that 100% of the active compound on the label is present in the tub.
2. **Heavy Metals:** ICP-MS screening for Lead, Cadmium, Arsenic, and Mercury ($<0.01\text{ ppm}$).
3. **Microbiological Safety:** Total plate count, yeast, mold, E. coli, and Salmonella clearance.
4. **Banned Substances:** Informed-Sport WADA screening for competitive athletes.

Customers can enter their batch number directly on the website to view the official Certificate of Analysis (CoA).

## 4. 9-Category Customer Feedback Engine (Voice-of-Customer)

All inbound customer reviews, support inquiries, returns, and social comments are classified into 9 operational categories:

1. **Taste / Flavor:** Feedback on sweetness profile, natural stevia balance, and flavor accuracy.
2. **Mixability:** Dissolution speed in cold water and foam settling time.
3. **Packaging:** Canister seal integrity, scoop accessibility, and moisture protection.
4. **Price / Value:** Perception of unit cost vs serving count.
5. **Delivery / Shipping:** Courier transit times, box condition upon arrival.
6. **Product Quality:** Texture consistency, clump resistance, and freshness.
7. **Product Understanding:** Customer comprehension of how and when to use.
8. **Website Experience:** Ease of quiz navigation, checkout smoothness, and subscription portal.
9. **Subscription Experience:** Flexibility of skip/pause actions and replenishment accuracy.
