## 3. The 5-Part Short-Form Video Post Architecture

Every video produced for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts follows this high-retention structural blueprint:

```mermaid
graph LR
    H[1. HOOK 0-3s] --> P[2. PROBLEM / QUESTION 3-7s]
    P --> V[3. VALUE & EVIDENCE 7-18s]
    V --> PAY[4. PAYOFF & APPLICATION 18-24s]
    PAY --> INT[5. NATURAL INTEGRATION 24-28s]
    INT --> CTA[6. INTENTIONAL CTA 28-32s]
```

### Script Architecture Blueprint:
1. **HOOK (0:00 – 0:03):** Pattern interrupt, contrarian assertion, or visual curiosity.
2. **PROBLEM / QUESTION (0:03 – 0:07):** Identifies a visceral frustration experienced by athletes (e.g. caffeine jitters, poor hydration cramps).
3. **VALUE & EVIDENCE (0:07 – 0:18):** Actionable physiological explanation citing mechanisms without medical jargon.
4. **PAYOFF (0:18 – 0:24):** Clear, practical protocol the viewer can execute immediately.
5. **PRODUCT INTEGRATION (0:24 – 0:28):** Natural demonstration of how Performance Lab formulated for this exact outcome.
6. **INTENTIONAL CTA (0:28 – 0:32):** Soft commercial action (*"Save this for your next leg day"* or *"Build your personalized profile in the bio"*).
