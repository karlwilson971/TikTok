## 1. Active Ingredient Scientific Compendium

### 🧪 Pure Fermented L-Citrulline
- **Clinical Effective Dose:** $6,000\text{ mg} - 8,000\text{ mg}$
- **Mechanism:** Bypasses splanchnic extraction in the liver to directly elevate systemic L-Arginine, fueling endothelial nitric oxide synthase ($eNOS$).
- **Key Human Study:** *Perez-Guisado et al. (J Strength Cond Res, 2010)* demonstrated a 52.92% increase in bench press repetitions and a 40% decrease in muscle soreness at 24h/48h post-exercise.

### 🧪 Creapure® Micronized Creatine Monohydrate
- **Clinical Effective Dose:** $3,000\text{ mg} - 5,000\text{ mg}$ daily
- **Mechanism:** Donates high-energy phosphate groups to replenish cellular ATP stores during 1–10 second maximal explosive efforts.
- **Key Human Study:** *Rawson & Volek (J Strength Cond Res, 2003)* meta-analysis showed an average 8% increase in 1RM strength and 14% increase in maximal repetitions to fatigue over control groups.

### 🧪 Magnesium Bisglycinate Chelate
- **Clinical Effective Dose:** $200\text{ mg} - 400\text{ mg}$ elemental magnesium
- **Mechanism:** Magnesium bound to two molecules of glycine; absorbed intact via intestinal dipeptide transport channels rather than competing for ionic mineral receptors.
- **Key Human Study:** *Abbasi et al. (J Res Med Sci, 2012)* showed significant increases in sleep time, sleep efficiency, and melatonin concentrations with concomitant reductions in serum cortisol.

### 🧪 Montmorency Tart Cherry Extract (*Prunus cerasus*)
- **Clinical Effective Dose:** $480\text{ mg} - 1,200\text{ mg}$ (equivalent to 50–90 whole cherries)
- **Mechanism:** Rich in anthocyanins and phenolic acids that inhibit cyclooxygenase ($COX-1$ and $COX-2$) enzymes, mitigating exercise-induced inflammation and lipid peroxidation.
- **Key Human Study:** *Howatson et al. (Eur J Appl Physiol, 2010)* found marathon runners consuming tart cherry experienced significantly faster recovery of isometric muscle strength and lower inflammatory biomarkers ($IL-6$).

### 🧪 Alpha-GPC (L-Alpha Glycerylphosphorylcholine)
- **Clinical Effective Dose:** $300\text{ mg} - 600\text{ mg}$
- **Mechanism:** Crosses the blood-brain barrier to serve as an immediate metabolic precursor for acetylcholine synthesis, boosting neuromuscular signaling and peak power output.
- **Key Human Study:** *Bellar et al. (J Int Soc Sports Nutr, 2015)* demonstrated a 14% increase in isometric bench press power output compared to placebo.
