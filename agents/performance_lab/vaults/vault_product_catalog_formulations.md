## 2. Clinical Ingredient Formulation Breakdown

### 1. Clean Focus Pre-Workout (`PL-PRE-01`)
- **6,000 mg Pure Fermented L-Citrulline:** Direct precursor to nitric oxide ($NO$). Increases plasma arginine levels higher than direct arginine supplementation, maximizing muscular vasodilation and time-to-exhaustion.
- **2,500 mg BetaPower® Betaine Anhydrous:** Cellular osmolyte supporting intramuscular hydration, cellular volume, and rate of force development during heavy compound lifts.
- **600 mg Alpha-GPC (50% Active = 300mg Choline):** Crosses the blood-brain barrier to synthesize acetylcholine, the primary neurotransmitter governing neuromuscular firing and mental focus.
- **175 mg PurCaf® Organic Green Coffee Caffeine:** Standardized botanical caffeine for smooth, steady alertness without the harsh peaks and valleys of synthetic anhydrous caffeine.
- **175 mg Suntheanine® L-Theanine:** 1:1 synergistic ratio with caffeine; promotes alpha brainwave activity to eliminate peripheral vasoconstriction and jitters.
- **300 mg Pink Himalayan Salt (120mg Sodium):** Rapid electrolyte replenishment for optimal nerve conduction during maximal muscle contractions.

### 2. Cellular Hydration Matrix (`PL-HYD-01`)
- **500 mg Sodium (Pink Himalayan Rock Salt):** Matches average sodium loss per 45–60 minutes of intense sweating; drives active glucose-sodium cotransport across intestinal walls.
- **200 mg Potassium (as Potassium Citrate):** Maintains intracellular fluid volume and prevents hyper-excitability of muscle membranes (cramping).
- **60 mg Magnesium Bisglycinate Chelate:** Bound to glycine for maximal intestinal bioavailability without osmotic diarrhea associated with cheap magnesium oxide.
- **1,000 mg Organic Freeze-Dried Coconut Water Powder:** Natural micronutrient co-factors and antioxidant support.
- **100 mg Aquamin® Marine Multimineral Complex:** Sourced from calcified red marine algae (*Lithothamnion calcareum*), delivering 72 bio-active marine trace minerals.

### 3. Creapure® Micronized Creatine (`PL-CRE-01`)

Micronized to 200 mesh for instant cold-water suspension.

### 5. Deep Recovery Magnesium & Tart Cherry (`PL-REC-01`)
