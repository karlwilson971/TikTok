## 3. The 8 Core Philosophy Pillars

1. **Consistency:** Small, deliberate daily habits compound into monumental physical adaptations.
2. **Performance:** Measure outcomes by speed, power, stamina, and mental acuity—not vanity metrics.
3. **Progress:** The only relevant benchmark is yesterday's baseline.
4. **Knowledge:** An educated athlete makes superior nutritional decisions.
5. **Discipline:** Motivation is fleeting; structured routines sustain progress.
6. **Longevity:** Fueling the body to perform at 60 as cleanly as at 25.
7. **Recovery:** Training creates the stimulus; sleep and nutrition build the adaptation.
8. **Intelligent Training:** Working with physiological systems rather than burning them out with stimulant overload.
