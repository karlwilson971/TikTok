## 3. TikTok Shop 1-Page Creator Brief Template

```markdown
# [Brand Name] TikTok Creator Brief & Guide

### 🎯 Objective
Create an engaging, authentic 20-35 second video showing how [Product Name] solves [Specific Problem] in a satisfying, relatable way.

### 🔑 Key Requirements
1. **First 2 Seconds Hook**: Show the product in motion immediately or start with a strong question/visual hook.
2. **Natural Lighting & Audio**: Crisp visuals and clear voiceover or trending sound.
3. **Anchor the Yellow Basket**: Tag the product via TikTok Shop Affiliate tool before publishing.
4. **Call to Action**: End with: "Grabbed mine from the yellow basket below!"

### 🚫 Prohibited (TikTok Shop Compliance)
- Do NOT make medical, permanent, or unverified claims.
- Do NOT cover the yellow basket area with text overlays.
- Do NOT mention external platforms (Amazon, Instagram, etc.).

### 💡 Top Hook Suggestions
- "Why did nobody tell me about this TikTok Shop find sooner?"
- "POV: You finally found the solution to [problem]..."
- "Testing if this viral organizer is actually worth the hype..."
```
