# SOP: The TikTok Commerce Flywheel Operating System (5-Agent Closed Loop)

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs the end-to-end execution of a content-first, high-converting, and policy-compliant TikTok Shop ecommerce brand.

---

## Phase 1: Market & Product Intelligence (`Agent 1`)
1. **100-Point Validation**: Run candidate products through the 11-dimension scorecard. Only products scoring $\ge 80/100$ enter the production pipeline.
2. **Catalog Tiering**: Categorize every item into Hero ($45\%$), Supporting ($25\%$), Repeat ($20\%$), or Trend Spike ($10\%$).
3. **Unit Economics Lock**: Verify gross margin $\ge 65\%$ and net contribution margin $\ge 28\%$ before sample ordering.

---

## Phase 2: Brand Positioning & Follower Magnet Strategy (`Agent 2`)
1. **Follower Magnet Standard**: Every piece of content must provide standalone value so viewers follow even if they never purchase.
2. **70/20/10 Content Mix**: Rotate weekly output: $70\%$ Value/Entertainment, $20\%$ Product-Integrated, $10\%$ Direct Commercial.
3. **6-Pillar Architecture**: Rotate across Desk Setup Transformations, Ergonomic Health, Habit Architecture, Behind-the-Scenes Craft, Skeptical Experiments, and Community Solutions.

---

## Phase 3: TikTok-Native Creative Production (`Agent 3`)
1. **Curiosity Hook Rule**: First 2.5 seconds must eliminate boring intros and deliver immediate visual motion, contrarian statements, or pattern interrupts.
2. **15-Point Concept Blueprints**: Write multi-variant scripts (Hook A/B/C) with precise on-screen text, voiceover timecode, visual cues, and TikTok Safe Zone alignment.
3. **Rolling 30-Day Pipeline**: Maintain 30 scheduled concepts ready for filming and asset assembly.

---

## Phase 4: Pre-Publishing QA & Policy Verification (`Agent 5`)
1. **9-Point Pre-Flight Audit**: Verify claims supportability, TikTok Shop policy, accurate pricing, compliant before/after visuals, zero prohibited health terms, stock levels $\ge 100$, and working product card anchors.
2. **Commercial Sound Clearance**: Ensure audio originates from the TikTok Commercial Audio Library (CAL) or original voice tracks to prevent video muting.
3. **Yellow Basket Tagging**: Confirm exact SKU attachment, discount badges, and active promotion alignment.

---

## Phase 5: TikTok Shop Growth & Creator Engine (`Agent 4`)
1. **7-Stage Funnel Telemetry**: Monitor Views $\rightarrow$ Profile $\rightarrow$ Clicks $\rightarrow$ ATC $\rightarrow$ Checkout $\rightarrow$ Purchase $\rightarrow$ Repeat.
2. **Failure-Mode Triage**: Detect bottlenecks instantly (High Views/Low Clicks $\rightarrow$ Fix CTA; High Clicks/Low Sales $\rightarrow$ Fix Offer/Price).
3. **Micro-Creator Scaling**: Seed 15–25 samples weekly to high-engagement micro-creators (10k–100k followers) using 3-tier commission incentives (15%, 20%, 25%).

---

## Phase 6: Post-Publishing Performance & VOC Feedback Loop (`Agent 5`)
1. **Weekly WIN / LEARN / KILL Report**: Every Monday, categorize all active content. Scale WINs via Spark Ads, iterate single variables on LEARN concepts, and retire KILL formats.
2. **5-Factor Root Cause Diagnostic**: Rigorously separate *Bad Product* from *Bad Creative*, *Bad Offer*, *Bad Audience*, or *Bad Execution*.
3. **Voice-of-Customer (VOC) Engine**: Continuously convert TikTok comments, customer reviews, objections, and returns into fresh video briefs and viral hooks for Agent 3.
